## 3. Current Landing Page Analysis

**URL:** [sugiicoaching.com/vsl](https://www.sugiicoaching.com/vsl)

### Headline & Positioning
- **Main Headline:** *"Drop the Fat, Build the Body, Keep Your Lifestyle"*
- **Sub-headline / Program Name:** *The L.E.A.N. Lifestyle System* — helps busy professionals get lean and stay lean without losing their freedom.

### Page Structure (VSL Format)
1. **CTA Button** — "Click to Sign Up" (top of page)
2. **Step 1** — Watch the video below (VSL video embed)
3. **Step 2** — Book Your Free 30-Minute Strategy Call
4. **Social Proof Section** — "Success Stories From People Just Like You" (image-based testimonials)
5. **Body copy** — The copy currently on the page appears to be **placeholder text pulled from a trading/finance coaching template** (talks about trading strategies, DAX market, GBPUSD, etc.) — this seems to be a mistake or leftover from a template. The copy does NOT match the fitness positioning.

### Key Observations
- The VSL page is minimalist with a video + CTA structure (standard high-ticket coaching funnel)
- The testimonials are image-based (before/after or screenshot format)
- The body copy mismatch is a **critical issue** — it undermines trust if a visitor scrolls past the video
- Target audience: **busy professionals** wanting fat loss without lifestyle sacrifice
- Offer: Free 30-min strategy call → presumably leading to a high-ticket coaching offer
- No pricing visible on page (typical for high-ticket discovery call funnels)

## 6. Competitor Reference — GOH Consulting

**URL:** [gohconsulting.com/training](https://gohconsulting.com/training)  
**Founder:** Cruz Brooks

### What GOH Does Well (Inspiration Points)

**Headline & Hook:**
- *"We will help you install our $230k/mo Sales Funnel"* — bold, specific, outcome-focused
- Targets coaches & consultants above $30k/mo — very specific niche qualifier

**Page Structure:**
1. Strong hero section with founder image
2. Video (Step 1: Watch The Video)
3. CTA — "IM READY TO SCALE" (Calendly booking link) — repeated multiple times
4. **"What You're Gonna Get On This Strategy Call"** — 3 concrete deliverables with icons:
   - Personalized breakdown & audit of current situation to pinpoint main bottleneck
   - Complete walkthrough of backend systems (scaled to $2M → $5M in sales)
   - Personalized roadmap to achieve goals step by step
5. **"Over 100 Successful Case Studies"** — grid of real result screenshots
6. Named case study highlights with specific revenue numbers

**Named Case Studies (Social Proof Format):**
- Andrew scaled to **$113k/mo**
- Alek scaled from **$0 to $137k/mo**
- Danny & Jayden: **$8k to $55k/mo in 90 days**
- Trey Howe: **$15k to $45k in 30 days**
- Matt: **$8k to $50k/mo in 30 days**
- Josh: **$500 to $50k/mo**
- Abhishek: **$11k to $45k/mo in 30 days**

### Key Takeaways to Adapt for Sugii

| GOH Element | Sugii Adaptation |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Bold specific claim ("$230k/mo funnel") | Bold transformation result ("Lost X lbs in Y weeks while working 60h/week") |
| "What you'll get on the call" section | Add 3 concrete deliverables for Sugii's strategy call |
| Named case studies with specific numbers | Use client first name + specific result (e.g., "Tom lost 12kg in 8 weeks") |
| Repeated CTA button | Repeat "Book Free Call" CTA 3–4 times down the page |
| Founder authority positioning | Highlight UK Championship win + transformation story |
